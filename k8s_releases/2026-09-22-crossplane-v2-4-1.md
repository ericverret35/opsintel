**Version**: v2.4.1
**Type**: Stable

### Changelog

`v2.4.1` is a patch release scoped to fixing issues reported by users of Crossplane `v2.4` and fixing security related issues in Crossplane and its dependencies.

# 🎉 Highlights

* **Package revisions now take control of established objects from the revision they replace:** An outgoing package revision could fail to relinquish control of the package's CRDs, leaving the incoming revision permanently unhealthy with `cannot establish control of object`. The provider's pod would never be created, so that provider could do no work without manual intervention by someone either deleting the stale revision or editing the CRD's owner reference by hand.
