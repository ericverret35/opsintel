**Version**: v1.20.15

### Changelog

`v1.20.15` is a patch release scoped to fixing issues reported by users of Crossplane `v1.20` and fixing security related issues in Crossplane's dependencies.

# 🚨 v1.20 end-of-life (EOL)

This is the final release of Crossplane `v1.20`. With the release of Crossplane `v2.5`, `v1.20` has reached its end-of-life and will no longer receive any support or maintenance by the Crossplane project, as announced in the [v2.4.0 release notes](https://github.com/crossplane/crossplane/releases/tag/v2.4.0).

Upgrade your control planes to Crossplane v2 to keep receiving fixes. Run `crossplane beta upgrade check` from the `v1.20` Crossplane CLI to find out if your control plane is affected by any of the [breaking changes in v2](https://docs.crossplane.io/latest/guides/upgrade-to-crossplane-v2/#rem
