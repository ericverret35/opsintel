### Changelog

`v2.4.3` is a patch release scoped to fixing issues reported by users of Crossplane `v2.4` and fixing security related issues in Crossplane and its dependencies.

# 🎉 Highlights

* **Distrust of compromised eMudhra intermediate CAs:** Bumps the Go toolchain to `1.26.10`, whose `crypto/x509` rejects the compromised eMudhra intermediate certificates (CVE-2026-111943), and removes the `AAA Certificate Services` and `emSign ECC Root CA - G3` roots they chain to from the image's CA bundle (#7957).
* **Truncated package downloads are no longer cached:** A package download that failed partway could be cached as a valid but truncated `package.yaml`.
