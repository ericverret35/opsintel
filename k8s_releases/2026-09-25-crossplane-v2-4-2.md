### Changelog

`v2.4.2` is a patch release scoped to fixing issues reported by users of Crossplane `v2.4` and fixing security related issues in Crossplane and its dependencies.

## 🎉 Highlights

* Fixed a bug where ownership of `ServiceAccount`s was not correctly transferred to newly active package revisions (#7838). Fixed in #7839 and backported in #7852.
