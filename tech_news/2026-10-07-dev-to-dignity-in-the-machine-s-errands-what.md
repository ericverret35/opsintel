**Source**: Dev.to

 There is a sentence circulating in tech circles that reads like a joke until you sit with it: AI agents are hiring humans. The words describe something genuinely new — software with a goal, paying a person for what it cannot do itself: stand somewhere, look at something, take a photo — and something very old. Somebody needs a thing done in the physical world. Somebody else does it. Money changes hands.
