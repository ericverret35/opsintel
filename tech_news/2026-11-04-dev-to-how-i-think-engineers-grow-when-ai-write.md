## How I think engineers grow when AI writes the first draft

**Source**: Dev.to

 For most of my career an engineer learned the craft by doing the small things first. Fix the typo in the error message, then the off-by-one, then the feature behind a flag, and somewhere in the second year the design of a whole module. AI coding tools now write a decent first draft of most of those small things, and I think that quietly removes the ladder a lot of us climbed. 

 I do not think the answer is to keep people away from the tools. It is to be deliberate about where the learning now
