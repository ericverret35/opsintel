## Designing Useful Support Answers for Pre-Purchase Questions

**Source**: Dev.to

 A customer-support assistant sits between changing store facts and a shopper who expects a clear answer. That makes the design problem closer to knowledge operations than to prompt writing. 

 This article applies that discipline to  pre-purchase support . The practical goal is: Cover product fit, compatibility, materials, care, shipping, and explicit uncertainty. The same method is useful whether the first implementation is a spreadsheet, an internal tool, or an AI-assisted support product.
