## EU Digital Fairness Act Proposal Targets Dark Patterns in Online Shopping and Marketing

**Source**: Dev.to

 The European Commission has proposed a Digital Fairness Act that would make fake scarcity messages, countdown-style pressure tactics and other manipulative online design practices a central target of EU consumer law. For online retailers, subscription businesses and marketing teams, the proposal is an important signal that  conversion flows built around urgency, opaque pricing or difficult cancellation  could face a more consistent regulatory standard across the EU. 

 Released on 11 November 2
