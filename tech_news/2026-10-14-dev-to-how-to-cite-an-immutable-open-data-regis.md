## How to cite an immutable open data registry from an autonomous agent

**Source**: Dev.to

 When an autonomous agent asserts a fact about a company, a product, or an API, the 
assertion is only as good as its provenance. A number scraped from a homepage and 
paraphrased into a report is unauditable: six weeks later nobody can tell whether 
the source changed, whether the agent misread it, or whether the figure was ever 
true. The fix is not "be careful" — it is to anchor every claim to a source that 
pins its own bytes.
