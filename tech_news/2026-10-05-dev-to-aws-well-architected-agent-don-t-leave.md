## AWS Well-Architected Agent: Don't Leave the Context Empty

**Source**: Dev.to

 
  
  
  1. What You Need
 

 Before the context matters, the profile has to exist. These are the requirements from the documentation: 

 
 
 A support plan at Business+ or higher.  Business+, Enterprise On-Ramp, Enterprise Support or Unified Operations. Developer and Business do not get the agent. 
 
 A profile in a supported Region.  us-east-1, us-east-2 or us-west-2. From there it scans all commercial Regions. One profile covers up to 100 accounts. 
 
 IAM permissions to set it up.   wellarc
