## How Incident Response Automation Reduces MTTR

**Source**: Dev.to

     

 When a production system goes down, every minute matters. Users may experience failed requests, delayed transactions, or complete service interruptions. Meanwhile, engineers need to identify what went wrong, understand its impact, and restore normal operations as quickly as possible.  

 This is where incident response automation becomes useful. Instead of relying entirely on manual investigation and repetitive operational tasks, teams can automate specific steps in the response process.
