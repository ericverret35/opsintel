## Database as a service (DBaaS): what it is, what it costs and when to self-host

**Source**: Dev.to

 Database as a service (DBaaS) means a provider runs the database and you get a connection string. The provider owns the servers and the disks. It installs and patches the database software, takes the backups and replaces a machine that fails. 

 You still design the tables, write the queries and choose how big the database is. You pay a monthly price, usually for an instance size plus the storage it holds.
