## I opened every free Amazon image compliance checker I could find — including my own

**Source**: Dev.to

 A rejected main image is one of the few Amazon listing errors you can catch  before  submitting — pure white background, 85% frame fill, 1000px+ for zoom, no text or watermarks. Yet most sellers only learn the rules after a rejection email. 

 So I opened every free compliance checker I could find and tested the same things on each: 

 
 Does the page actually have a working upload? (I checked the rendered HTML for  type="file" , not just the marketing copy) 
 Does the image stay on your device
