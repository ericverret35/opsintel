## Your AI agent re-reads its tool list on every step — that's 32% of the bill

**Source**: Dev.to

 You give your agent a toolbox: read a file, run the tests, search the web. The model picks a tool, your code runs it, the result goes back, the model picks the next one. It feels like the model  knows  its tools. It doesn't. It forgets them after every reply, and you pay to remind it. 

 I counted what that reminder costs on a realistic toolbox. Short version: about a third of the bill.
