## I built a shadowing player for English that waits until you finish speaking

**Source**: Dev.to

 I made Practice Shadowing on my own and launched it in October 2026. This post is about what it does and what I learned building it. 

 What it does 
Shadowing is repeating what a speaker says straight after them. Practice Shadowing plays one sentence, then waits until you have finished speaking before it goes on. While you speak, the words you say light up. Afterwards you can play your attempt next to the speaker's and see your rhythm.
