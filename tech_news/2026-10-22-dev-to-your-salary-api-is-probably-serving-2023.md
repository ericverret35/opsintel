## Your salary API is probably serving 2023 data. I refreshed 907 rows and measured the drift.

**Source**: Dev.to

 Last week I finished reloading every wage figure in a salary/take-home product I run ( kultranz.com ) from a 2023-vintage dataset onto the newest BLS release, the Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) survey with a  May 2025 reference period . I kept both vintages in version control, so when it was done I could diff old table against new and answer a question that bothered me: how far off is a calculator that just... never updated? 

 Further than I expected.
