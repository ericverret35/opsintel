## We translated our terminal game into Portuguese. Except the terminal.

**Source**: Dev.to

  DockerLinux  is a free game for learning Linux and Docker: a simulated terminal in the browser, 10 levels of missions, on-call "boss levels" where a pretend production server is broken and you have to fix it. 

 A lot of players are Brazilian, so this week it got its first second language:  Brazilian Portuguese . The interesting part wasn't what we translated. It was what we didn't. 

     

 
  
  
  The rule: translate everything  around  the terminal, never the terminal
