## No Signal, No Problem: How to Build Apps for the Field

**Source**: Dev.to

 A farmer standing in a field at 6 a.m. has gloves on, sun glaring off the screen, and barely any cell signal. They need to log a pest sighting, check today's weather alert, and move on. The whole interaction should take seconds.  

 Most farm apps are built as if the user is at a desk with fast Wi-Fi. That disconnect is why adoption numbers in agriculture stay frustratingly low; one study of German farmers found 95% owned smartphones, but only 71% used the crop app built specifically for them.
