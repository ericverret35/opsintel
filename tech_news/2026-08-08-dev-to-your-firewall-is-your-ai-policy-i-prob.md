## Your firewall is your AI policy — I probed 18 major sites to read it

**Source**: Dev.to

 Everyone's arguing about AI search visibility. Almost nobody is measuring the boring part: whether AI crawlers can  fetch your pages at all . 

 So I built a small open-source tool —  geo-crawl-audit  — that probes any site with the user-agents of every AI crawler that matters (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, ChatGPT-User, and friends), measures how each is treated versus a normal browser, and checks the thing most people never think about:  how many words exist in the raw HTML
