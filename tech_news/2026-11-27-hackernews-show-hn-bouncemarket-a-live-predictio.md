## Show HN: bouncemarket – a live prediction market game for a bouncing ball

**Source**: HackerNews

 a ball will fall. trade on where it lands. I made this game! Some technical details: The entire drop of the ball is simulated server-side, then streamed to each client as server-sent events with a small buffer. Every drop is deterministic from its starting position, so rounds can be replayed exactly for later analysis, but tiny variations in starting position can change the ultimate outcome.
