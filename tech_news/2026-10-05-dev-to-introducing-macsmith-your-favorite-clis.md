## Introducing Macsmith: Your favorite CLIs. One native Mac GUI.

**Source**: Dev.to

 A typical local development setup on a Mac involves several tools: Homebrew for packages, mise or nvm for runtimes, Docker for containers, and databases running in the background. 

 Each tool has its own commands. When you need to check what’s running, switch a runtime, or find the process occupying a port, you move between them. 

  Macsmith brings those tools into one native Mac GUI.  

 I’m sharing Macsmith with the developer community and would love feedback from people who work with these
