## EU CCAT Project Develops Open-Source Tools for Continuous Security Assessment

**Source**: Dev.to

 The EU-funded Cybersecurity Certification and Assessment Tools project, known as  CCAT , is developing an  open-source toolchain  for continuous security assessment of digital products and services. Coordinated by Masaryk University under Horizon Europe, the project aims to help producers, consumers and regulators assess cybersecurity in the context of evolving EU rules, including the Cybersecurity Act and Cyber Resilience Act.
