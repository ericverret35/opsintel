## Two serious security issues in PHP and the update coming on October 21

**Source**: Dev.to

 Two high-severity flaws rated 9.2 out of 10, referred to as PHP2Shell, have been disclosed. 

 PHP announced a patch release for October 21 at 14:00 UTC, with no active attacks reported. 

 Affected versions and internal components have not yet been disclosed, while CERT-EU advises isolating internet-facing applications until updates are applied.
