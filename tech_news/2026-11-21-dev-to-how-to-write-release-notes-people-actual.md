## How to Write Release Notes People Actually Read

**Source**: Dev.to

 People read release notes that tell them what got better for them. Group the notes by what people do in your app, lead every line with what they get, and leave out anything they never saw. Your AI can draft all of it from your pull requests. The  free AI starter kit  we build our own products with writes notes this way on every release.
