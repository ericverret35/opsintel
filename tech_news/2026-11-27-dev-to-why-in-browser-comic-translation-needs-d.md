## Why In-Browser Comic Translation Needs Dynamic Typesetting Instead of Plain Text Overlays

**Source**: Dev.to

 When translating standard web pages or technical articles, replacing text is straightforward. You locate a DOM node, swap its innerText with the translated phrase, and let normal CSS reflow handle line breaks and margins. 

 Comics, webtoons, and manga do not work that way. Every piece of dialogue lives inside a drawn speech balloon with fixed geometric boundaries. Some balloons are tall vertical ovals, common in Japanese print manga. Others are wide horizontal bubbles typical in Korean webtoon
