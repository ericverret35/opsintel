## WooCommerce Variations Not Showing? A 10-Step Debugging Checklist

**Source**: Dev.to

 You add sizes or colors to a WooCommerce product, save it, and open the page. No dropdowns. Or the dropdowns show but a choice is greyed out. Or you get "Sorry, no products matched your selection." 

 After fixing this on many stores, I run the same checks in the same order. Start with settings, then templates and conflicts, then JavaScript and code. Jumping straight to deactivating plugins wastes time. 

 Back up your database and files first, and test on staging if you can.
