## Modeling Chair Utilization as a Finite Resource in Dental Chair Scheduling Software

**Source**: Dev.to

 
  
  
  The chair, not the calendar, is the constraint
 

 A dental practice does not really sell appointments. It sells chairs with a clinician, an assistant and an instrument tray attached, and two bookings at 10:00 are compatible only if the chairs are — something a calendar has no way of knowing.
