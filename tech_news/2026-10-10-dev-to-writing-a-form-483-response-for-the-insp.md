## Writing a Form 483 response for the inspector is a mistake — write it for your own team

**Source**: Dev.to

 There are two ways to write a Form 483 response. One is for the inspector who issued it. The other is for your own team eighteen months from now, when the follow-up inspection arrives or the warning letter escalates. The first version is faster. The second is harder to write and easier to defend. If you only have time for one, write the second.
