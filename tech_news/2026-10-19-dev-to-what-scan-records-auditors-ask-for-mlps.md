## What scan records auditors ask for (MLPS / supply chain)

**Source**: Dev.to

 Audit week asks for “security scan records,” and the folder only has green CI screenshots—no policy version, no frozen report, no who approved exceptions. Questionnaires want an  evidence chain , not the sentence “we have a scanner.” 

 
  Takeaway:  Keep four record types: (1) gate policy and changes; (2) completed scan jobs (with engine/rule versions); (3) frozen report snapshots (published external copies when needed); (4) suppressions/exceptions and share-access traces. AngusSecurity provid
