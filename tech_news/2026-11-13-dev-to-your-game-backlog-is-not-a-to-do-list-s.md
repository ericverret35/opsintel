## Your game backlog is not a to-do list. Stop treating it like one.

**Source**: Dev.to

 In 129 real game backlogs, 84% of games never leave "Backlog". Maybe the list is doing a different job than you think. 

 You will never finish your game backlog, and the data says that's fine. I run a free  game backlog tracker  and looked at 129 real accounts (25,276 games, 2026-11-10): 84% of games are still marked Backlog, 11.5% are Done. The median backlog is 64 games and would take about 1,091 hours. 

 A to-do list is supposed to reach zero. A backlog doesn't, and it was never going to.
