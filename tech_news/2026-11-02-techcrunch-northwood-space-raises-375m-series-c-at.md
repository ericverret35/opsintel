## Northwood Space raises $375M Series C at a $2B valuation from a16z and DFJ growth

**Source**: TechCrunch

Space infrastructure company Northwood Space on Monday said it had raised $375 million in a Series C round co-led by Andreessen Horowitz and DFJ Growth, valuing the company at $2 billion.
