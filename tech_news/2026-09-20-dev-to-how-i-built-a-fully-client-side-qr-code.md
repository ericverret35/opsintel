## How I Built a Fully Client-Side QR Code Generator (And Why I Avoided a Backend)

**Source**: Dev.to

  The Problem with Most QR Generators  

 I was building a simple tool for my own use and needed to generate a QR code for a WiFi network. I tried the popular free tools, and they all did the same thing: 

 
  They routed the QR code through their own server. 
  They set an expiry date (7 days, 30 days, etc.). 
  They charged you to keep the QR code alive after that. 
 

 I was frustrated. A QR code is just a pattern of black and white squares that encodes a URL.  Why does it need to expire?
