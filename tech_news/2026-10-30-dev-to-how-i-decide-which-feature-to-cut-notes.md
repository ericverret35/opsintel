## How I decide which feature to cut (notes from building Tingbase)

**Source**: Dev.to

 Every project I've shipped or tried to ship hits the same point: something has to go, and it's usually a thing I enjoyed building. 

 I'm making Tingbase in Unity 6 and C#. It's a 3v3 hero shooter where you swap between Tank, DPS and Healer. The core sentence for it is short: pick a role, enter a match, play until it ends. Anything that doesn't serve that sentence is a candidate for the cut. 

 
  
  
  The one question I ask first
 

 If this feature goes, what does the player actually lose?
