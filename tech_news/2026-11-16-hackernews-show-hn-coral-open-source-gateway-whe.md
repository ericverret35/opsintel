## Show HN: Coral – Open-source gateway where agents write their own tools

**Source**: HackerNews

 Hi HN, I'm James, one of the co-founders of Coral. Coral is an open-source, self-hosted gateway that sits between your agents and the systems they work on. Agents call tools by writing short TypeScript programs that they can save as custom tools to reuse. You can publish custom tools for groups of agents, so your team’s library of tools adapts to your unique systems, data and use cases.
