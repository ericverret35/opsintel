## Your cutout passes on white and fails on dark: a 10-line edge test before you ship product photos

**Source**: Dev.to

 Most product-photo pipelines are tuned on a white background, because that is what marketplaces ask for. White is also the most forgiving background a cutout can be tested on. Every mistake a matte makes — a contaminated edge, a one-pixel ring of the old background, a bite taken out of a hair strand — disappears against white and reappears the moment the same PNG lands on a dark card, a coloured hero, or a comparison table. 

 That is not a rendering bug. It is what the file actually contains.
