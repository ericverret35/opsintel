## I built my friend a voice-based recipe note-taking app

**Source**: Dev.to

 
  
  
  What I built
 

 Built a browser-based app which takes audio input from the microphone and transcribes it into recipe notes/memo. 

 
  
  
  Building for a Friend
 

 Made it for my friend, Arnesh. He is very passionate about cooking and I built this app so that he can note down recipe notes/memoes hands-free when his hands are occupied while cooking.
