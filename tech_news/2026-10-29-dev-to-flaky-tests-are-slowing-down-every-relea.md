## Flaky Tests Are Slowing Down Every Release: Here's How to Fix Them

**Source**: Dev.to

 The build fails. You re-run it. It passes. Nobody investigates, and the team moves on. 

 That small ritual is how  flaky tests  quietly drain a team. A flaky test passes and fails on the same code, so every failure becomes a question: is this a real bug, or just noise? Once engineers stop trusting the answer, the whole test suite loses its value. 

 
  
  
  Why tests turn flaky
 

 
 
 Brittle selectors:  A renamed button or a restructured page breaks tests that were never really wrong.
