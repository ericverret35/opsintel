## WhatsApp doesn't translate calls. Here's what does, and where your audio goes

**Source**: Dev.to

 If you're trying to talk to a partner's parents, or your own, on WhatsApp and you 
don't share a language, the short answer is that WhatsApp won't help with the call 
itself. It translates messages. Calls happen in whatever language people speak. 

 There are ways around that, and they differ more than the vendor pages make it look. 
The thing that differs most is what the person on the other end has to do, and the 
thing almost nobody mentions is where the call audio ends up.
