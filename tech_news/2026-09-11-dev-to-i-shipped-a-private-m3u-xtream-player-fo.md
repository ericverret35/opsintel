## I shipped a private M3U/Xtream player for Apple

**Source**: Dev.to

 I built  Tifo  — a private M3U / Xtream / Stalker playlist player for iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV. It can also talk to Plex, Jellyfin, and Emby. 

 Tifo does  not  include any channels or streams. You bring your own playlists or media servers. Think VLC energy: playback + library UX, not a content service. 

 Technical bits include a VLC engine alongside native playback, EPG, custom icons, split view / multi-view, PiP, and AirPlay. There are no ads, no tracking, and no account.
