## Writing honest 'coming soon' placeholders instead of shipping fake features

**Source**: Dev.to

 Every tool site eventually has a feature that is not ready yet. On ramrom.io, that feature is Douyin. The download provider I looked at returns playlists, not files, so there is no honest way to offer a Douyin download today. I had three options: hide the platform entirely, ship a button that pretends to work, or put up a plain "coming soon" note. I chose the third, and I think it is the only one that respects the visitor.
