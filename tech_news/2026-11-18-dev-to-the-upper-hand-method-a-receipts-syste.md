## The Upper Hand Method - a receipts system for working with an AI

**Source**: Dev.to

 The Upper Hand Method is a free blueprint (CC BY 4.0) for working with an AI assistant on things that matter — decisions, money, commitments — without losing the ability to check its work, or your own. 

 It's a folder of plain-text files and five mechanisms: 

 
 Pre-commitments with falsifiers — before a decision that matters, write the decision, your expectation, your confidence, and the evidence that would prove you wrong. 
 Graded ledgers — every checkable call logged when made, graded on
