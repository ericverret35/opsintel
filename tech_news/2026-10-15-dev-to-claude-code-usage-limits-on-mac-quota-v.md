**Source**: Dev.to

 A Claude Code token counter can show how much activity was recorded on your Mac. It cannot, by itself, tell you how much of your Claude subscription allowance remains or when your limit resets. 

 If you need to decide whether you can finish a coding task before the next reset, check the account quota. If you want to investigate where your tokens went, inspect a token report. Those readings answer different questions.
