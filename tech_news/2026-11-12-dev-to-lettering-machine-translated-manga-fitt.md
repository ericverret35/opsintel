## Lettering machine-translated manga: fitting text back into speech bubbles

**Source**: Dev.to

 When people think about translating manga automatically, they think about the translation model. In practice the translation is the easy part. The hard part is everything around it: finding the text, getting it off the page cleanly, and putting the translation back so the page still reads like a comic. 

 This post walks through the image side of an automatic manga translator I work on, and what we learned by comparing our pages with official English and Korean editions.
