## Decide what meeting-room AI must run on the device

**Source**: Dev.to

  Tormod Ree , chief product and engineering officer at Neat, describes meeting rooms with 11 or more cameras and dozens of microphones. The system has to tell who is present, who is talking, and who is looking at whom, then choose what a remote participant sees and hears. That choice sits on the live media path. Where you run it is the design decision.
