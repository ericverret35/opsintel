## Four Tenant Isolation Rules for SaaS Help Center Retrieval Explained

**Source**: Dev.to

 The hard part of a multi-tenant help center is not producing an answer from a PDF. It is proving that the retrieved passages belong to the requesting tenant before latency pressure turns a shortcut into a disclosure.
