## Found w2s_ Admin Users in WordPress? Your Site Was Hit by wp2shell

**Source**: Dev.to

 If a random admin account showed up in WordPress with a username starting with  w2s_  or a  wp2shell.local  email address, your site was hit by the wp2shell exploit. 

 These accounts are created by automated attack scripts going after a critical vulnerability in WordPress core, and you need to lock down and clean up the site right away. Don't just delete the user and walk away. The underlying flaw may still be open, and your password hashes may already be exposed.
