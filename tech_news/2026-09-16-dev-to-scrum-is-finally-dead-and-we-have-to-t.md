## Scrum is finally dead 🎉 and we have to thank Coding Agents for that

**Source**: Dev.to

 
  
  
  The funeral nobody is sad about
 

 Let's be honest about something we've all been pretending not to notice. 

  Scrum is dead.  

 And almost nobody is sad about it. 

 We've spent twenty years standing in a circle every morning, moving tickets across a board, arguing about whether something is a 3 or a 5, and calling it "agility." We built an entire industry on top of it — certifications, coaches, tooling, dashboards. We turned a lightweight idea into a bureaucracy with a mascot.
