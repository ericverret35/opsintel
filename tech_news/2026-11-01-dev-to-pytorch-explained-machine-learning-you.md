## PyTorch Explained: Machine Learning You Can Watch Learn

**Source**: Dev.to

 TensorFlow showed one way to train a model, with layers stacked and compiled. PyTorch, released by Meta's AI research lab in 2016, takes a more hands-on approach: you write ordinary Python, tensors track their own gradients, and the training loop is a loop you can read, print from, and change. That openness made it the favourite of researchers, and it is now just as common in production, from image models to language models.
