**Source**: Dev.to

 A freelance rate can look healthy on paper and still leave you short at the end of the year. 

 The usual trap is dividing a hoped-for annual income by 2,080 working hours. That assumes every weekday is paid client work. In reality, some of your time goes to proposals, admin, learning, bookkeeping, holidays, and quiet periods. Your minimum rate needs to account for the hours you can realistically bill—not every hour you are awake or at your desk.
