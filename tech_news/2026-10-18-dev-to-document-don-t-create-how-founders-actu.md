## Document don't create: how founders actually do it

**Source**: Dev.to

 document don't create is the idea that you should share what you are actually doing, as you do it, instead of trying to invent content about it. you are not making an advert or a lesson. you are keeping a record of the work, out loud, and letting people watch it happen. for a founder that means the decisions, the problems and the small wins of building the thing, told while they are still in motion.
