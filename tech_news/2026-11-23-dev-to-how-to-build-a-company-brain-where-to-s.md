## How to build a company brain: where to start and what it costs

**Source**: Dev.to

 To build a company brain, start with one workflow where missing context hurts, such as product and engineering alignment meetings. Capture those conversations automatically, give them permissions that match who was in the room, and serve them to the agents doing the follow-up work. Prove that one use case, then add sources.
