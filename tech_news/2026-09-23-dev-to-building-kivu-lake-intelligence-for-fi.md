## Building KIVU — Lake Intelligence for Fish-Cage Farmers on Lake Victoria

**Source**: Dev.to

 Fish cages on Lake Victoria can lose an entire stock overnight when dissolved oxygen crashes. Most farmers still rely on experience, visual checks, and late phone calls. We wanted something better. 

 KIVU is a lake-wide aquaculture and environmental intelligence platform we built as a team of five.
