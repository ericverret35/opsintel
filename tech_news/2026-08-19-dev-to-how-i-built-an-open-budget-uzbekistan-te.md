## How I built an Open Budget Uzbekistan Telegram bot with AI captcha solving

**Source**: Dev.to

 Open Budget Uzbekistan is a government platform where citizens vote  
for public initiative projects (parks, roads, lighting, etc.) in  
their districts. Each year, thousands of projects compete for budget  
funding based on public votes. 

 I built a Telegram bot that automates the voting process and rewards  
participants with UZS (Uzbek Som) for each vote they submit.
