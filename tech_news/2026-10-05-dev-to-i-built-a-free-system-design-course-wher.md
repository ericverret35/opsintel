## I built a free system design course where your architecture has to survive simulated traffic

**Source**: Dev.to

I've spent years on backends with Kafka, Postgres and Kubernetes, and the lessons that stuck with me were always the ones where something broke in production. 

 So I built  ArchPath , a free course where you learn system design by running traffic through your own architecture.
