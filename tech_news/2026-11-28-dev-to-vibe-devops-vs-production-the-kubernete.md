## Vibe DevOps vs Production: The Kubernetes Iceberg

**Source**: Dev.to

  Originally published on  Naveed Ahmed's Engineering Blog .  

 You've heard of vibe coding. 

 Describe what you want, let AI generate it, and keep prompting until the application runs. 

 Now imagine applying that same approach to infrastructure. 

 "Write a Dockerfile." 

 "Create a Kubernetes Deployment." 

 "Generate a Terraform module." 

 Everything looks good in a local test. 

 But then production traffic arrives. 

 Welcome to what I call  Vibe DevOps .
