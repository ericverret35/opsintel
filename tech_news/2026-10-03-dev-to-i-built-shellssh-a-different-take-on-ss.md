## I Built ShellSSH: A Different Take on SSH Clients Like Termius, Termux and PuTTY

**Source**: Dev.to

 SSH itself doesn't need reinventing. 

 The workflow around SSH might. 

 For years, my typical server workflow looked something like this: 
 

 
  Find server credentials
        ↓
Open SSH client
        ↓
Connect
        ↓
Need a file?
        ↓
Open SFTP client
        ↓
Need another server?
        ↓
Find another profile
        ↓
Something looks wrong?
        ↓
Connect and start checking
