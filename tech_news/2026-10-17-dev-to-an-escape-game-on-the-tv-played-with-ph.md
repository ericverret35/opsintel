## An escape game on the TV, played with phones: keeping 5 screens in sync

**Source**: Dev.to

 Since early October I've had  Nocturne au musée  online: an escape game you play together in front of the TV. The TV (or a computer) shows the museum, each player joins by scanning a QR code with their phone, and gets documents the others don't have. To move forward, you have to talk to each other. The game is in French, but the architecture isn't.
