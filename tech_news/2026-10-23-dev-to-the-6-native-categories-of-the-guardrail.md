## The 6 native categories of the guardrail

**Source**: Dev.to

 
  
  
  Short answer
 

 The six native categories of IBM’s Granite guardrails are:  harmful content ,  privacy ,  bias and fairness ,  robustness ,  transparency , and  accountability . These categories structure the model’s built-in safety controls to align with enterprise AI governance standards. 

 
  
  
  TL;DR
 

 
 Granite guardrails are pre-configured, domain-agnostic safety layers embedded in IBM’s foundation models.
 
 All six categories are implemented at inference time via runtime
