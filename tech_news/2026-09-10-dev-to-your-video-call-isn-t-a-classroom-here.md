## Your Video Call Isn’t A Classroom. Here’s What It Is.

**Source**: Dev.to

     
Every ed-tech team hits the same wall eventually: your video call works fine for a meeting, but a classroom needs more. A teacher who can promote a student's mic, a whiteboard that stays in sync for forty kids, breakout rooms that don't fall over. Building that stack from raw WebRTC is a semester-long project by itself. So instead of building it, I set out to stand up Agora's  Flexible Classroom  (the desktop build) and see how far a&nbsp;.env file and an afternoon could actually get me.
