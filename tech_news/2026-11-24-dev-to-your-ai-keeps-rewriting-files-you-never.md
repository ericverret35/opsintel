## Your AI keeps rewriting files you never asked it to touch? Constrain it.

**Source**: Dev.to

 Vibe coding's most annoying failure mode isn't a wrong answer. It's an AI that "helpfully" rewrites three files you never mentioned while fixing the one you did. 

 The fix is pattern #3 from our Prompt Starter Kit:  constrain it.  

 Before the AI writes anything, draw the fence: 

 
 What may change 
 What may not change 
 What should make it stop and ask 
 

 The template we teach in Lesson 1: 
 

 
  Only change [this function]. Touch nothing else. If the fix needs more, tell me first.
