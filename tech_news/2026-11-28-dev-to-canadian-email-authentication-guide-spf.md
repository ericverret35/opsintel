## Canadian Email Authentication Guide: SPF, DMARC & Zero-PII Compliance (CCCS ITSP.40.065, PIPEDA & Law 25)

**Source**: Dev.to

 For Canadian IT teams, municipalities, law firms, and MSPs, email domain protection sits at the intersection of two requirements:  anti-spoofing enforcement  and  data minimization . 

 On the technical side, the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security's  ITSP.40.065  ( Implementation Guidance: Email Domain Protection ) recommends implementing  SPF, DKIM, and DMARC  together and progressing toward enforcement ( p=quarantine  and  p=reject ) so unauthorized senders cannot impersonate your domain.
