## How AI Image Generators Work: I Trained a Diffusion Model From Scratch on My Laptop

**Source**: Dev.to

 I trained an AI to draw on my laptop, from scratch. At the start it could only make noise. About 35 minutes later it was drawing robots that never existed. 

 It works the same way as Stable Diffusion and Midjourney: the model learns to turn noise into pictures. Then I let it train itself on its own best drawings, and it learned to cheat. 

 This post walks through the whole thing: the code, the math, and the real numbers from my run. The full video lesson is here:  https://youtu.be/19Omz2cwFuY
